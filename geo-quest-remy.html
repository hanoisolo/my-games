How it works

Answer 10 geography questions. Tap the answer you think is right — big buttons, no rush, no timers.

Stuck? Tap Need a nudge? for a clue. Still stuck? You can knock out two wrong answers — but using a hint means no star for that question. Stars have to be earned!

A wrong pick just brings a kind explanation and a similar question to try. Mistakes are part of learning.

Progress is saved only on this device.
