How it works

Each question walks you through the steps: spot the signs, pick the right rule, and (for take-aways) rewrite the question as adding the opposite — then solve. Stuck on a step? Tap Need a nudge? for a hint that guides you without giving the answer.

Stars are earned by solving a question with no hints and no wrong steps. Using a hint still counts as solved — it just doesn't earn a star. They have to be earned!
